Need Immediate Help?

If you are experiencing overwhelming emotional distress, feel in immediate danger, or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out right away. You are not alone, and compassionate, free help is available 24/7 across India.

National Emergency Number Call 112 All-in-one Emergency (Medical • Police • Rescue)
Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India) Call 14416 Toll-free 24/7 National Tele-Mental Health
KIRAN Helpline (Govt. of India) 1800-599-0019 24/7 Toll-Free Psychological Support
Vandrevala Foundation +91 9999 666 555 24/7 Free Crisis Counseling by Psychologists

Note: ThriveSpace does not provide emergency intervention, automatic calling, or location tracking. You remain in complete control.
